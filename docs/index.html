At-risk SKUs ·

Projected cover = (stock on hand + open orders) ÷ forecast daily demand. A SKU is at risk when that is shorter than its supplier's promised lead time. Bars are coloured by supplier status.

No SKU-location is at risk for this selection.

Portfolio project by Chiamaka Ikpo. All data is synthetic and generated by scripts/generate_data.py. KPI definitions follow the KPI dictionary: OTIF = received lines on or before the promised date and in full ÷ received lines · Lead-time variance = standard deviation of days late · Stockout rate = SKU-location-days with zero stock ÷ all SKU-location-days · Excess = stock above the class maximum cover, at unit cost.
